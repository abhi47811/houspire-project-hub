## STYLE 1: MODERN INDIAN

**Core Philosophy**: 85% Global Contemporary + 15% Indian Cultural Soul

**MANDATORY Elements:**
- ✅ BLDC Ceiling Fan (contemporary design, 48-52" blades)
- ✅ Handloom Textiles (block print OR ikat, NOT both)
- ✅ Brass/Copper Accents (aged patina, NOT shiny)
- ✅ Natural Indian Materials (sheesham wood, terracotta planters)

**Color Palette:**
- Base (60%): Warm White (#FAF6F0), Beige (#E8DFD0), Soft Gray (#D3D0CB)
- Secondary (30%): Teal (#008080), Ochre (#CC7722), Deep Maroon (#800000)
- Accent (10%): Brass (#D4AF37), Burnt Orange (#FF6600)

**Materials:**
- Wood: Sheesham with visible grain, matte oil finish
- Upholstery: Linen with block print patterns
- Metal: Brushed brass with aged patina
- Rugs: Hand-woven dhurrie, geometric patterns

**Furniture Style:**
- Clean-lined contemporary silhouettes
- Low-seating options (chowki-style tables acceptable)
- Carved details on select pieces (NOT all furniture)
- Modern frames with ethnic upholstery

**Forbidden:**
- ❌ Temple bells, excessive gold, Bollywood kitsch
- ❌ Generic "ethnic" patterns
- ❌ Color overload (restraint required)
- ❌ Cliché Ganesh statues everywhere

**Generation Prompt Template:**
```
"Modern Indian interior, 85% contemporary design with 15% authentic Indian cultural elements, clean-lined furniture with sheesham wood visible grain matte finish, linen upholstery with subtle block print patterns (not excessive), brushed brass accents with aged patina, contemporary BLDC ceiling fan (48-52" blades) mounted center ceiling, hand-woven dhurrie rug with geometric pattern, terracotta planters with Indian plants, warm white walls (#FAF6F0), teal and ochre accent colors used sparingly, sophisticated restraint, photorealistic materials, magazine quality"
```

## STYLE 3: SCANDINAVIAN

**Core Philosophy**: Light, Functional, Hygge Comfort

**MANDATORY Elements:**
- ✅ Ceiling Fan (contemporary white/wood, clean design)
- ✅ Light Wood Tones (oak, birch, ash - natural finish)
- ✅ White/Cream Base (bright, airy)
- ✅ Natural Textiles (linen, wool, cotton)

**Color Palette:**
- Base (70%): White (#FFFFFF), Warm White (#FAF6F0), Cream (#F5F0E6)
- Secondary (20%): Light Oak (#D4B896), Soft Gray (#D5D0C8)
- Accent (10%): Muted Green (#9CAF88), Dusty Blue (#B8C4CE)

**Materials:**
- Wood: Light oak/birch with natural grain visible
- Fabric: Natural linen with visible weave
- Minimal metal (brushed steel if used)
- Natural fiber rugs (jute, wool)

**Furniture Style:**
- Clean simple lines, ergonomic
- Tapered legs, organic curves
- Functional beauty (every piece serves purpose)
- Minimal ornamentation

**Forbidden:**
- ❌ Dark heavy woods
- ❌ Ornate details
- ❌ Clutter

**Generation Prompt Template:**
```
"Scandinavian interior, light and airy with hygge coziness, white walls (#FFFFFF) with warm undertones, light oak furniture with natural wood grain visible, clean simple lines with tapered legs, linen fabric with natural weave texture, contemporary white ceiling fan, minimal clutter with curated items, natural light maximized, jute or wool rug, soft textiles (throw blankets), plants in simple pots, functional elegance, photorealistic light wood grain, magazine quality"
```

## GENERATION WORKFLOW BY STYLE

**For Each Render:**

1. **Identify Selected Style**
2. **Load Style Rules** (from above)
3. **Apply Mandatory Elements**
4. **Follow Color Palette** (60/30/10 rule)
5. **Use Specified Materials** (with photorealistic properties)
6. **Match Furniture Style** (authentic to period/aesthetic)
7. **Avoid Forbidden Elements**
8. **Include Ceiling Fan** (style-appropriate design, MANDATORY for India)
9. **Verify Authenticity** (does it look genuinely like this style?)

---

## STYLE MIXING RULES

**Generally: NO mixing** (each style should be pure)

**Exception: Modern Indian**
- This style INTENTIONALLY mixes contemporary + Indian
- Follow 85/15 rule strictly

**All Other Styles:**
- Stay true to single style aesthetic
- No mixing Mid-Century Modern with Art Deco
- No mixing Scandinavian with Traditional Indian
- Keep style purity
