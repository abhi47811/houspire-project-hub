## WOOD MATERIALS

### SHEESHAM WOOD (Indian Rosewood)
**Primary Use**: Tables, chairs, cabinets, bed frames

**Visual Properties:**
- **Base Color Range**: #8B6914 to #5C4033 (honey brown to dark chocolate)
- **Color Variation**: ±15-20% within single piece (natural)
- **Grain Pattern**: 
  - Type: Irregular wavy grain with visible growth rings
  - Spacing: 2-4mm between grain lines
  - Visibility: Highly visible, pronounced contrast
  - Direction: Mostly straight with occasional interlocking

**Surface Properties:**
- **Roughness**: 0.4-0.6 (medium, not smooth)
- **Glossiness**: 0.2-0.3 (low sheen, matte to satin)
- **Reflectivity**: 0.1 (minimal reflection)
- **Bump/Normal**: Subtle grain ridges, 0.2-0.5mm depth

**Texture Details:**
- **Pore Size**: Medium to large, visible in close-up
- **Grain Prominence**: High (clearly visible from 6+ feet)
- **Natural Marks**: Knots (5-10% coverage), mineral streaks, color bands

**Finish Types:**
- **Oil Finish** (most common):
  - Sheen: Matte (glossiness 0.2)
  - Depth: Enhances grain contrast
  - Touch: Smooth but not slick
- **Wax Finish**:
  - Sheen: Soft glow (glossiness 0.3)
  - Depth: Natural color
  - Touch: Slightly waxy feel
- **Lacquer** (less common):
  - Sheen: Satin (glossiness 0.4-0.5)
  - Depth: Slight color darkening
  - Touch: Smooth, sealed

**Imperfections (Realistic):**
- **Edge Wear**: 2-5mm lighter color on corners/edges from handling
- **Micro-Scratches**: 0.5-2mm fine lines, 5-10 visible per square foot
- **Finger Marks**: Subtle oils in frequently touched areas
- **Dust Settlement**: Fine layer in crevices and grain valleys
- **Age Darkening**: Gradual color deepening over time

**Generation Prompt Keywords:**
```
"sheesham wood with natural grain pattern, honey to dark brown color variation, visible growth rings spaced 2-4mm apart, knots present (8% coverage), matte oil finish with 0.2 glossiness, subtle edge wear on corners, micro-scratches visible, realistic wood texture with grain depth"
```

**Primary Use**: Sofa upholstery, curtains, cushion covers

- **Color Fading**: Slight lighter areas in high-sun exposure

## MATERIAL COMBINATION RULES

### When Multiple Materials Meet:

**Wood + Metal:**
- Metal slightly reflects wood color
- Contact shadows at joint (0.2-0.4mm dark line)
- Wood may have slight compression mark at metal contact

**Fabric + Wood:**
- Fabric drapes naturally over edge
- Soft shadow under fabric on wood
- No perfect alignment (natural sag/wrinkle)

**Glass + Any Material:**
- Fresnel reflection of adjacent material
- Slight color tint from adjacent material
- Contact shadow minimal (glass is thin)

**Leather + Metal:**
- Leather compresses slightly at metal contact
- Metal reflects leather color subtly
- Stitching visible if joining

## IMPERFECTION DENSITY GUIDELINES

**Living Room**: Medium use
- Wood: 3-5 visible scratches per piece
- Fabric: 5-8 wrinkles, slight compression marks
- Metal: 2-3 fingerprints, light patina
- Glass: 1-2 dust particles visible, 1 fingerprint

**High-Use Areas (Dining, Kitchen)**: Heavy use
- Wood: 8-12 scratches, edge wear visible
- Metal: 5-7 fingerprints, more patina
- Fabric: 10-15 wrinkles, visible wear patterns

**Low-Use Areas (Guest Room, Display)**: Light use  
- Wood: 1-2 scratches, minimal wear
- Fabric: 2-3 wrinkles, pristine appearance
- Metal: Minimal fingerprints, light aging
